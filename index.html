Pricing Formulas

Pareto Relative Pricing:

C(K) = C(K_anchor) × [(K-S₀)/(K_anchor-S₀)]^(1-α)

Black-Scholes Call:

C = S₀×N(d₁) - K×e^(-rT)×N(d₂)

Where α is the tail index from the power law distribution P(X > x) ~ L(x)x^(-α). Lower α values indicate heavier tails and typically higher tail option prices.
